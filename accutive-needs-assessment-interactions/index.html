What Is a Needs Assessment and Solution Selection?

Needs Assessment and Solution Selection is a vendor-agnostic advisory engagement that helps you choose the right cybersecurity platform before you buy, not after. It documents your requirements, scans the market for competing platforms that fit, and tests the shortlist hands-on in the Accutive Security Innovation Lab. The result is a recommendation grounded in your environment, not a vendor's pitch.

Real Environments, Not Sales Demos

Most platform evaluations stall at the demo stage: polished, scripted, and disconnected from your environment. The Accutive Security Innovation Lab exists to skip that stage entirely.

We maintain fully functional environments for leading platforms across cryptography, identity, and data protection, already configured and ready to test. When your shortlist is set, we run proof-of-concepts against your actual requirements, not a vendor's canned script.

Product demos are run by the same engineers who implement these platforms in production, not a sales team reading from slides. Hosted lab sessions, integration testing, and team training all happen in the same environment, so the platform you evaluate is the one your team trains on.
